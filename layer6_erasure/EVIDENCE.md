# Layer 6 erasure: evidence on fresh seeds and at scale

MOCK stores generated by our own seeder (the organisers' real layout is not available). Fresh random seeds the agent was never developed on. 'completeness' = seeded copies removed; 'silent' = copies left behind that the agent did NOT flag for a human; 'collateral' = other borrowers' or decoy records deleted by mistake. Limit: 15 minutes (900 s) per erasure request.
